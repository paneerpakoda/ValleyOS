## Day 1

6AM - Wake up
pick up parsnips
![[Pasted image 20260420201726.png]]

![[Pasted image 20260421205505.png]]


by 8:10AM, collect 50 wood
craft chest and put everything other than tools inside
by 8:30AM, go top left exit and forage the way
forage around Robins House by 8 50 you should reach robins house and directly go towards community centre if there are no foragables left - dont go near the river
Forage around Community Centre
start with weed to the left of CC
Go back to Bus Stand and collect any forage-ables
go back to city centre and whack all the weeds
starting from top right and going down 
then go towards marnie
forage around the forest
again start from top right and go anticlockwise
now go back near haleys house and keep foraging 
go to beach next
Keep scythe with you for harvesting weeds and hoe for taking any artifact that might appear
if you do find an artifact before 4PM - donate to gunther for 250g rewards
this can be used to buy additional parsnips before his store closes 
Save one of each forageable to turn them into 10 seeds - 70 gold more than selling them individually 
Go back to farm - 6PM
get as many parsnips planted as possible while also leaving some time to clear the path to the southern exit of your farm
dont be afraid to eat whatever forageable you found for energy just make sure you are efficient with usage
_At the end of the day, do not put anything in the bin_
